DOTFALL ARENA

iPhone · Free with optional Remove Ads

Survival sandbox of pure chaos

Hundreds of little dots. One arena. Everything in it wants them gone. Build the arena, unleash 50 hazards and the hunters, then watch who survives. Or grab any dot and fight back yourself.

  • Autonomous dots that dodge, team up, panic and evolve, with real physics behind every hit
  • 50 hazards, deadly combinations and a director that keeps the pressure coming
  • Build blocks, ramps and walls while the arena runs
  • Take control of any dot, or follow it and watch it fight
