DOTFALL ARENA

iPhone · Free with optional Remove Ads

A never-ending 3D survival arena. Crowds of autonomous dots dodge a director that throws 50 kinds of hazards at them, while you release hunters, reshape the map and watch who survives the longest.

  • Real rigid-body physics: collisions, gravity, knockback and tumbling wreckage
  • Dots evolve as they survive, through Adapted, Elite, Apex and Ascendant, and gain new abilities
  • 25 autonomous hunters, six handcrafted maps plus seeded random arenas
  • Take control of any dot, or follow it and watch it fight
