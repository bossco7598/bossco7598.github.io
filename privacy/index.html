Privacy Policy

DOTFALL ARENA for iOS · Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information is involved when you use DOTFALL ARENA ("Dotfall", "the app"), made by Bossco ("I", "me"). The short version: I don't run accounts or servers for Dotfall and I don't collect your personal information. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Information I collect

None. Dotfall has no sign-up, login or account. I do not collect, receive or store your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. The game simulation runs entirely on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

Dotfall saves a few preferences locally on your iPhone so they persist between launches, for example sound levels, the population and arena preset you unlocked, card positions and sizes, and whether you have purchased Remove Ads. This data stays on your device, is not sent to me, and is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Dotfall shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including rewarded "watch to unlock" ads and full-screen ads between play sessions. To deliver, limit, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt;
  • Device and usage data, such as device model, operating system version, app version, IP address (from which Google may infer approximate location), ad interactions and app interaction data;
  • Diagnostics, such as crash, performance and ad-delivery data.

Google acts under its own privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. I do not receive any of this data in a form that identifies you; AdMob only gives me aggregated reports such as total ad impressions and earnings.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS, Dotfall asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you may still see ads, which will be less personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Dotfall uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form before personalized ads are served. You can review or change your choices later with the Ad privacy choices button in Arena settings → Arena → Ads & purchases, when it is shown in your region.

4. In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. I never see your payment details, Apple ID or billing information. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase is active, and stores a local flag so ads stay removed. Purchases are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

Dotfall is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact me and I will help address it.

6. Sharing and selling

I don't sell or share personal information, because I don't collect any. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's policies linked above.

7. Your choices

  • Allow or deny tracking in the ATT prompt or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change ad consent with Ad privacy choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop ads from being shown.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
