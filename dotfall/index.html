DOTFALL ARENA

For iPhone · Survival sandbox of pure chaos

Hundreds of little dots. One arena. Everything in it wants them gone. DOTFALL ARENA is a survival sandbox you can watch, shape and play. The dots think for themselves: they dodge, team up, panic, grab power-ups and chat about their odds. You decide how much chaos they face.

Watch a living arena

  • A crowd of autonomous dots fights to stay alive in real time, with physics behind every bump, launch and fall
  • Tiny chat bubbles show what they're thinking ("Better keep my distance.")
  • Slow it down to 0.5× or speed it up to 4×

Unleash the chaos

  • 50 hazards: spinning arms, meteors, presses, chain mines, ricochet turrets, gravity wells, laser sweeps and plenty of stranger ones
  • Hunters with their own tricks, from the Prism Heron to the Entropy Alchemist
  • Combination events chain hazards together: The Blender, Pinball Panic, Hot Crossfire, Meteor Shower, The Car Wash
  • A director keeps the pressure coming. Tune its intensity, hazard frequency, wave size and combinations

Build the arena

  • Drop climbable blocks, ramps and walls straight into a running arena
  • Rotate, erase and undo as you go, and watch the dots adapt to your layout

Take control

  • Pick any dot and pilot it yourself with a touch joystick
  • Dash, strike and shoot your way through the hazards
  • If your dot falls, take over the next one and keep going

Every dot has a story

  • Tap a dot to open its card: combat record, survival stats, body and instincts
  • Dots evolve as they survive, gaining levels and abilities
  • Follow a favorite with the camera, or skip to the next survivor
  • Cards are resizable and movable, so you keep the arena in view

Who lasts longest?

  • The survival board ranks the living legends by time alive, kills, damage dealt and taken, power-ups and health
  • The Hall of Fame keeps the greatest survivors
  • The crown holder is always the longest survivor alive

Choose the battlefield

  • 6 themed maps: The Foundry, Glacier Nine, Sunken Colosseum, Neon Switchyard, Reactor Zero, Overgrown Vault
  • 4 arena sizes, from Classic to Colossal
  • Presets for every mood: Custom, Expanded, Gentle chaos, Mayhem, Cataclysm and Moon bounce (low gravity)
  • Set the population, arrival rate and reinforcements yourself

Free to play

DOTFALL ARENA is a free download. It is supported by optional ads: a short break now and then, and a short video to unlock map, preset and population changes. A one-time Remove Ads purchase removes every ad and every lock for good, and you can restore it on any device with the same Apple ID. See Support and the privacy policy.

No account needed. Everything runs on your device.
