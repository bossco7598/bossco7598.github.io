DOTFALL ARENA

For iPhone

A perpetual survival arena. Autonomous dots run, dodge, fight and evolve while a relentless director combines hazards against them. How long can the oldest survivor last?

What's inside

  • Real 3D physics. Contacts, gravity, bounce, impulses and moving machines. Wreckage tumbles, slides and reacts to explosions and gravity fields.
  • A hazard director. 50 procedural hazard types combined through warm-up, hunt, overdrive and breather phases. Tune intensity, frequency, hazards per wave and combinations.
  • Dots that learn and evolve. Survivors grow through Adapted, Elite, Apex and Ascendant, becoming Windrunners, Phasewalkers, Lifeblooms, Ironhearts or Mirages, each with its own ability.
  • 25 hunters. Brawlers, ranged hunters, trappers and ambushers, each with its own way of choosing and chasing prey.
  • Maps. The Foundry, Glacier Nine, Sunken Colosseum, Neon Switchyard, Reactor Zero and Overgrown Vault, plus seeded random arenas up to 4× wider and deeper.
  • Color alliances. Eight factions that group up, share warnings and healing, and avoid hitting each other.
  • Take control. Pilot any dot with dashes, strikes and a blaster, or follow it hands-off. When your dot dies, control passes to the next one.
  • Build the Arena. Place your own cover and obstacles, with cards you can move and resize anywhere on screen.
  • Live records. A survival board of the oldest living dots and fallen records, plus a full combat record for every dot.

Free, with an optional Remove Ads

Dotfall is free and supported by ads. A one-time Remove Ads purchase removes every ad and every unlock prompt. See Support for how ads and unlocks work, and the privacy policy for what data is involved.
