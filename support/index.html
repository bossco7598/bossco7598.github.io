Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?

Remove Ads is restored automatically when you reinstall Dotfall or use a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID. If it doesn't come back, open Arena settings → Arena → Ads & purchases and tap Restore purchases. You won't be charged again.

What does Remove Ads do?

It's a one-time purchase ($3.99 in the US) that removes every ad, including the full-screen ads and the watch-to-unlock prompts. Map, preset and population changes then apply instantly.

When do ads show?

In the free version, a full-screen ad plays about every 10 minutes of active play and when you reset the arena. Ads never play within 90 seconds of each other, never interrupt while your finger is on the screen, and the arena pauses while one plays. Time spent in menus or with the app in the background doesn't count.

How do watch-to-unlock changes work?

Some changes need one short rewarded ad in the free version:

  • Switching maps: browse maps freely; the ad plays when you tap Enter arena on a different map or different map settings. Re-entering the same map is free.
  • Switching the arena preset away from Custom: one ad per switch.
  • Population changes (Dots tab → Population): make as many changes as you like, then tap Apply · watch ad to apply them all together with one ad, or Discard to cancel.

The change only applies after the ad finishes. If you close the ad early, nothing changes.

It says "Ad not ready, try again in a moment"

An ad hasn't loaded yet, usually because of a slow or missing internet connection. Check your connection and tap Retry. The change stays locked until an ad plays, or you can buy Remove Ads to skip ads entirely.

How do I change ad tracking or consent?

Tracking permission is in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, use Ad privacy choices in Ads & purchases. See the privacy policy for details.

Do I need an account?

No. Dotfall has no accounts and runs entirely on your device.
